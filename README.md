# Layoffs_and_Hiring_Trend_Analysis
Excited to share one of my latest Power BI projects that analyzes how AI adoption is influencing hiring trends, layoffs, financial performance, and workforce transformation across industries.
This interactive dashboard converts complex workforce data into actionable business insights through dynamic visualizations, KPIs, and drill-down analysis.

🔹 Page 1: Layoffs & Hiring Trend Analysis
Key Metrics: 60M Total Layoffs | 35M Open Roles | Job Security Score: 5.81 | Employee Sentiment: 6.50
Insights:
Layoffs peaked at 20.31M in 2024, declined in 2025 (19.76M), and slightly recovered in 2026 (20.05M).
AI automation, restructuring, cost-cutting, and market slowdown contributed almost equally to workforce reductions.
Social Media recorded the highest layoffs, followed by AI, E-Commerce, Cybersecurity, Gaming, FinTech, and Cloud.

🤖 Page 2: AI Impact Dashboard
Key Metrics: AI Replacement Risk 7.21 | 7K High-Risk Companies | AI Adoption 5.54 | Automation Impact 6.38
Insights:
Leading tech companies including Google, OpenAI, NVIDIA, Meta, and Salesforce show high AI adoption alongside workforce restructuring.
AI replacement risk remains high across industries, with Social Media and E-Commerce most affected.
Higher AI adoption is strongly associated with workforce optimization.

💼 Page 3: Hiring Analysis
Key Metrics: 35M Open Roles | 50.21% Remote Jobs
Insights:
Open roles increased from 11.39M (2025) to 11.70M (2026).
Companies like Apple, Google, Amazon, OpenAI, and Netflix continue hiring.
Most in-demand roles include Machine Learning Engineer, Data Scientist, DevOps Engineer, Cloud Engineer, Software Engineer, Product Manager, and Cybersecurity Analyst.

📈 Page 4: Financial Performance vs Layoffs
Key Metrics: Revenue Growth 16.63% | Stock Growth 22.27% | Salary Budget Change 5.56% | Employee Sentiment 6.49
Insights:
Cybersecurity recorded the highest stock growth.
Positive revenue growth despite layoffs suggests improved operational efficiency.
Salary budgets increased across major technology companies while employee sentiment remained moderately positive.

🛠 Tools & Skills
Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Data Modeling, KPI Cards, Maps, Treemaps, Waterfall, Scatter, Line & Pie Charts, Interactive Slicers, Data Storytelling.

📌 Business Takeaways
AI is transforming workforce strategies by reshaping skill demand rather than simply replacing jobs. Despite layoffs, hiring remains strong for AI, cloud, cybersecurity, and data roles.
